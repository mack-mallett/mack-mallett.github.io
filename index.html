What I do

I'm curious about the core challenges facing your business and if an AI or ML system can solve that problem at scale. I combine my background in business analysis and user experiences with a strong foundation in mathematics and software development to build and deploy data analysis and AI systems.

Professionally, I'm interested in what makes ML and AI systems successful at scale, graph theory and graph analysis techniques, understanding the information and dynamical systems theory behind successful systems, and where human insight can provide tangible value over AI systems.

I build fully connected data pipelines that apply these techniques, and design accessible UI's to make insights usable by everyone in the organization. Throughout my career I’ve worked in a variety of business verticals including digital marketing, construction, and IT service experience management.

You can find me based out of Victoria BC, where I'm pursuing a Masters of Engineering in Applied Data Science at the University of Victoria. If you'd like to chat, please send me a message on LinkedIn!
