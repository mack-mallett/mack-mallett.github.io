About Me

I'm a Data Scientist with experience in machine learning, graph analysis, and distributed systems. I build fully connected data pipelines that apply these techniques, and design accessible dashboards to make insights usable by everyone in the organization. Throughout my career I’ve worked in a variety of business verticals, developing strong business acumen. I’m keen to build on this experience to accomplish my goal of becoming an expert in my field.



You can find me based out of Victoria BC, where I'm pursuing a Masters of Engineering in Applied Data Science at the University of Victoria.


Some of my current interests are in: distributed computing (esspecially as it relates to distributed data pipelines and distributed machine learning/algorithms), incorporating ML and AI technologies into existing software and data reporting systems, unsupervised machine learning, and graph analysis techniques.
